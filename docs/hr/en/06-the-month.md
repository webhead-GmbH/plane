# The month, from open to closed

Every month goes through the same four steps: you hand it in, somebody agrees it, somebody closes it, and then it is finished. This page is for anybody who has to do one of those steps and has not been told what it means — what to press, in what order, what each step stops you doing afterwards, and what to do when something turns out to be wrong. It covers both sides: your own month on **My time**, and everybody's month on **Team time**.

## The five names a month goes by

A month is in one of five states. The same five words are used on both screens: on **My time** the state is a small badge with a clock beside it, under the three figures. On **Team time** it is the **State** column of the table.

| State             | What has happened                                                      | Can the hours still be changed? |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Not handed in** | The month has not been given in yet. This is where every month starts. | Yes                             |
| **Handed in**     | The person has pressed **Hand in**. It is waiting to be agreed.        | No                              |
| **Agreed**        | Somebody who looks after the team has agreed the figures are right.    | No                              |
| **Closed**        | The figures are frozen and are what goes to payroll.                   | No                              |
| **Reopened**      | A closed month that was put back so something could be corrected.      | Yes                             |

Two things about that list are worth reading twice.

**The hours stop being changeable at "Handed in", not at "Closed".** A month that has merely been handed in already refuses new hours, changes to absences, and changes to attendance. The screens say so with a sentence that mentions the month no longer being open — for example, in the day form: **"This month is no longer open, so its hours cannot be changed. Ask whoever looks after the team to reopen it if something needs correcting."**

**A reopened month never goes back to "Not handed in".** It goes to **Reopened**, and from there it has to be handed in, agreed and closed all over again. Both closings stay on the record. Nothing about the first one is erased.

## Your month, step by step

This is the part everybody in the company does, once a month, for their own hours.

### During the month

1. Open **My time** from the left-hand sidebar. You see the month you are in, three figures — **To work**, **Worked**, **Balance** — and a table with one row per day.
2. Add anything that no work item accounts for: meetings, admin, training, travel. Press **Record time for today**, or click the date of any day in the table. How to do that is on the page "Recording hours".
3. Hours you booked on work items with a timer are already counted. You do not enter those here.

There is no need to press **Bring up to date** in the "…" menu. The month is worked out again every time you open **My time**.

### After the month has ended

You cannot hand a month in while it is still running. Wait until the month is over, then:

1. Open **My time**. If it has already moved on to the new month, press the **‹** arrow beside the month name to step back to the month you are handing in.
2. Read the day table from top to bottom. Days you did not work show **Not a working day** and count nothing. Days you were away show **Away** or **Partly away**. Public holidays show **Public holiday** or **Half holiday**.
3. If any timers are still running on your work items, stop them. While one is running you will see, under the figures: **"A timer is still running on one of your work items, in this or another workspace. Stop it, then you can hand the month in."** The month cannot be handed in until it has stopped.
4. If a day carries an orange triangle, read [Days that need looking at](#days-that-need-looking-at). You cannot clear it yourself, and it does not stop you handing the month in.
5. Press **Hand in**.

### The box that appears

Pressing **Hand in** does not hand anything in yet. A box appears, headed **Hand in**, that says:

> Once March 2026 is handed in, you cannot change its hours, time off or attendance yourself. Ask whoever looks after the team if something needs correcting.

Press **Hand in** again to go ahead, or **Cancel** to change your mind.

When it has gone through, a green message appears: **Handed in**, and underneath, _"March 2026 is now waiting to be agreed."_ The badge under the figures changes to **Handed in**, and the **Hand in** button disappears from the screen. It is gone because there is nothing left to hand in, not because something has failed.

### When Hand in is grey

The button is there but cannot be pressed. Rest the mouse on it and it tells you why:

| What it says                                                                                                             | What to do                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **"The month is not over yet. It can be handed in once it has ended."**                                                  | Wait. It can be handed in on the first day of the next month.                                                                             |
| **"A timer is still running on one of your work items, in this or another workspace. Stop it, then hand the month in."** | Find the timer and stop it. It may be in a different workspace from the one you are looking at. "Hours in detail" lists the running ones. |

### When there is no Hand in button at all

Then the month has already been handed in, agreed or closed. Check the badge under the figures to see which.

## What handing in stops you doing

From the moment you press **Hand in**, and for as long as the month is **Handed in**, **Agreed** or **Closed**:

- You cannot add or remove hours on any day of that month. The day form shows a grey line instead of the boxes.
- You cannot add, change or remove the start, end or break of a working day in that month.
- Nobody can record a new absence in that month, and nobody can agree or refuse one that is still waiting. This is why every request for time off should be agreed or refused **before** the month is handed in.
- A timer on a work item dated into that month is refused, with: _"These hours fall in a month that has already been settled. Reopen it first if they genuinely belong there."_
- The month stops being worked out again. Nothing recorded into it afterwards will ever reach its figures.

If you try to change something anyway, you get a red message that names the month and its state, for example: **"March 2026 has been handed in, so its records can no longer change. Ask whoever looks after the team to reopen it on Team time."**

That is the right thing to do. Ask. Nothing is lost — the month can be reopened, and then corrected.

## Agreeing a month

Only somebody who looks after the team can agree a month, and only from **Team time**.

### Getting to Team time

There is no sidebar entry for it. Open **My time**, press the "…" button at the top right, and choose **Everyone's hours**. That item only appears for people who look after the team.

**Team time opens on last month, not this one.** That is the month people are handing in, so it is the useful one to land on. The month name is written out above the table; use the **‹** and **›** arrows to move to any other month.

### Agreeing

Before you agree anybody's month, go to the **Absences** page for that month and deal with every row that still says **Asked for**. A request that has not been agreed changes nothing at all — not the hours, not the balance, not the leave account — and once the month has been handed in it can no longer be agreed at all. See the page "Time away from work".

Then:

1. Find the person's row. Its **State** column reads **Handed in**, and the **Action** column offers a blue **Agree** button.
2. Check the figures on the row. What each one means is under [Where the figures come from](#where-the-figures-come-from).
3. Press **Agree**.

There is no confirmation box. The month is agreed the moment you press it. A green message says **Agreed**, the **State** column becomes **Agreed**, and the button becomes **Close**.

**Agreeing cannot be undone.** There is no step from **Agreed** back to **Handed in**. If you agree the wrong row, the only way back is to close that month and then reopen it with a written reason — and closing may itself be refused for other reasons. Read the name in the **Person** column before you press.

### You cannot agree your own month

Your own row appears in the table like everybody else's, and it offers the same blue **Agree** button. Pressing it always fails, with: _"Approving your own month is not a decision. Ask someone else."_

This is deliberate, and there is no way round it on the screen. If you are the only person who looks after the team, your own month can never be agreed, and therefore never closed, until a second person is given that role. That is set on the **People** page, with the switch **Looks after the team**. See "People and their terms".

You can, however, close and reopen your own month once somebody else has agreed it. Only agreeing is blocked.

## Closing a month

Closing is the last step. It is what makes the figures final and hands them to payroll.

### What closing does

1. It freezes the totals onto the month. From then on, those are the figures that are read — the month is never worked out again.
2. It writes down what the figures were worked out from: which contract, which working week, which holiday calendar, which absences, and the hours owed on every single day. That is what makes "why was March 161 hours" answerable a year later.
3. It marks the absences, the hours with no work item and the attendance records inside the month, so they refuse to be edited.
4. It fills in the **Running balance** for that person for the first time.

Most attempts to change a closed month are refused outright, and say so. A few are not: adding or removing a public holiday, for example, is accepted with a green message and then changes nothing for anybody whose month is closed. **A closed month keeps the figures it was closed with, whatever anybody changes afterwards.** If a correction genuinely has to reach it, the month has to be reopened first.

### Closing, step by step

1. Find a row whose **State** column reads **Agreed** and whose **Action** column offers a blue **Close** button.
2. Press **Close**. A box appears, headed for example **"Close Anna's month?"**, that says:

   > The figures are frozen as they are now. Nobody can change this month's hours or absences afterwards, and anything recorded into it later will not count. The month can be reopened, and the reopening is recorded.

3. Press **Close** to go ahead — it reads **Closing…** while it works — or **Cancel**.
4. When it has gone through, a green message says **Closed**. The **State** column becomes **Closed**, the **Running balance** column shows a figure for the first time, and the **Action** column becomes **Reopen**.

If the month refuses to close, the box stays open with the person's name still on it, and a red message explains why. Work through the next section.

## When closing is refused

These messages appear in English, whatever language the rest of the screen is in. They are listed here so they can be read in either language.

| The message                                                                                                                                                                   | What it means                                                                                                          | What to do                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Only an approved month can be closed."                                                                                                                                       | The month is not in **Agreed** — usually because somebody else has already acted on it and your screen is out of date. | The table refreshes itself after every action. Look at the **State** column again. |
| "This month is still running. It can be closed from 2026-04-01."                                                                                                              | The month has not ended yet. The date given is the first day it can be closed.                                         | Wait until that date.                                                              |
| "February 2026 has not been closed yet, and this month opens on what that one closes with. Close it first, or the balance carried into this month starts again from nothing." | Months are closed oldest first. See below.                                                                             | Step back to the month it names and close that one first.                          |
| "Some days are marked for review because hours counted earlier are no longer there. Settle those before closing the month."                                                   | At least one day still carries the orange flag.                                                                        | See [Days that need looking at](#days-that-need-looking-at).                       |
| "You don't have the required permissions."                                                                                                                                    | You do not look after the team.                                                                                        | Ask whoever does.                                                                  |

### Months are closed oldest first

Each month opens on the balance the month before it closed with. If February is not closed and you close March anyway, March would start again from nothing and everything since the beginning would be quietly thrown away. So the closing is refused until February is closed.

A month that holds nothing at all does not block anything. Merely paging back through old months on this screen creates empty months for everybody, including months from before people were employed — those do not count as unfinished. Nor does a month that ends before the person's starting balance takes effect. A month that has ever been closed does count, however empty it looks, because a later month was already opened on a figure it is now free to change.

One consequence worth knowing before you use "Bringing hours in from somewhere else": bringing in a year of old months puts all of those months into the chain. They then have to be handed in, agreed and closed in order, oldest first, before the current one can be closed.

### Somebody who is leaving

Somebody marked as having left disappears from the **Team time** table for every month, including the months they worked. Their final month can then no longer be handed in, agreed or closed from this screen. **Finish their last month first, and mark them as left afterwards.** The box that marks somebody as left says the same thing.

## Days that need looking at

Sometimes hours that a month had already counted are no longer there. The commonest cause is somebody deleting a work item, which takes every timer on it, including other people's. The system cannot tell whether the hours were withdrawn on purpose or lost by accident, so it refuses to guess: it flags the day and holds the month open until a person says what happened.

### How you know

- On **My time**, under the figures: **"Some days need looking at — hours that were counted before are no longer there. Whoever looks after the team has to settle them before the month can be closed."** The day itself carries an orange triangle in the **What it was** column.
- On **Team time**, an orange triangle appears beside the person's name, and the **Action** column offers **Look at those days** instead of anything else.

A flag does not stop a month being handed in and does not stop it being agreed. It stops it being closed. So an **Agreed** month with a flagged day shows **Look at those days** where the **Close** button would otherwise be.

### Settling a flagged day

Only somebody who looks after the team can do this, and only from **Team time**.

1. Press **Look at those days**, or the orange triangle beside the person's name.
2. A box opens, headed **"Days to look at for Anna"**. It explains: _"Hours that were counted on these days are no longer there. Writing down what happened lets the month be closed, but it does not put the hours back — if they should still count, have them recorded again first. What you write is kept with the day, and the person it is about can read it."_
3. Each flagged day is a card of its own. It shows the date, and on the right **"Counts as 6:00 now"** — the hours the day is left with after the loss.
4. Under **What happened?**, type what you found out. **Settle this day** cannot be pressed until there is something in the box. Ask the person before you write anything; the note is kept and they can read it.
5. Press **Settle this day**. A green message says **Day settled** and the card disappears.
6. Do the same for each remaining card. When the last one has gone, the box reads: **"Nothing left to look at. This month can be closed."**
7. Press **Done** to close the box. **Done** closes the box, not the month.

Settling changes no figure whatsoever. It does not put the hours back and does not take them off. It records, against that day, that somebody asked what happened and got an answer. If the hours should still be counted, they have to be recorded again on the work item, before the month is handed in.

## Reopening a month

**Read this before you do it.** Reopening is recorded with your name, the time and your reason, and cannot be hidden or taken back. The figures the month was closed with are kept alongside the new ones — the first closing stays readable, which is the point. But a closed month has usually already been sent to the payroll accountant, so reopening one means the file they hold is now out of date and somebody has to tell them.

### Reopening, step by step

1. On **Team time**, find the row whose **State** column reads **Closed**. The **Action** column offers **Reopen**.
2. Press **Reopen**. A box appears, headed for example **"Reopen March 2026?"**, that says: _"Anna's month has been closed and its figures frozen. Reopening keeps the closed version and records this one alongside it — nothing that was agreed is overwritten."_
3. Under **Why is it being reopened?**, type the reason. The example shown in the empty box is _"Three days in the second week were logged against the wrong month."_ Write something a person reading it in a year's time could act on.
4. **Reopen** cannot be pressed until the box has something in it. Press **Reopen**, or **Leave it closed** to change your mind.
5. A green message says **Reopened**. The **State** column becomes **Reopened**, and the **Action** column goes back to **Waiting for Anna to hand the month in**.

The month's hours, absences and attendance can be edited again. The person then corrects what was wrong, hands the month in again from **My time**, and it is agreed and closed a second time.

### Two things to watch

**Reopen the newest closed month first.** Each closed month keeps the running balance it was closed with, and reopening an older month does not go back and correct the months already closed after it. If March, April and May are all closed and March has to be corrected, reopen May, then April, then March, and close them again in the other order.

**The running balance shown on a reopened month is the old one.** Reopening does not clear the figure the month was closed with. So on **Team time** the **Running balance** column, and on **My time** the note **"-1:36 carried forward"** under **Balance**, still show what the first closing produced. The three figures above them are live and will move as things are corrected. The running balance is only rewritten when the month is closed again.

## Where the figures come from

The same four figures appear on both screens. Every one of them is held in minutes and shown as hours and minutes: **161:12** means 161 hours and 12 minutes, not 161.12 hours.

| Figure              | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **To work**         | The hours the contract says the person should work, added up over the days of the month, from their own working week. **Nothing ever reduces it** — not a public holiday, not a day of leave, not sickness.                                         |
| **Worked**          | Four things added together: hours on work items, hours with no work item, time off that counts as worked, and public holidays.                                                                                                                      |
| **Balance**         | **Worked** minus **To work**. Green when ahead, red when behind, grey at exactly **0:00**.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Running balance** | Where the person stands in total after this month: the balance they came into the month with, plus this month's **Balance**, minus any hours they took off in lieu. **It only gets a figure when the month is closed.** Until then it shows a dash. |

Two rules follow from that table, and both surprise people.

**Time off and public holidays are added to Worked; they are not taken off To work.** A week of paid leave shows as a full week of **Worked** hours nobody spent at a desk. The **Balance** comes out the same either way, but this way the hours figure handed to payroll is the number of hours the person is actually paid for. On **My time** the hint under **Worked** says so — **"includes 23:06 away or on holiday"**. On **Team time** the tile does not say it, so a manager reading **Worked** should remember what is in it. Somebody away for a whole month shows **Worked** equal to **To work** and a **Balance** of exactly **0:00**.

**A public holiday is only worth what the person would have worked that day.** On a day they never work it shows as **Not a working day** and adds nothing. Nothing has been taken from them. And for the two arrangements where somebody invoices us for their own hours, no hours are expected each day at all: their **To work** and **Balance** are **0:00** for ever, and a public holiday credits them nothing. That is correct and is not a failure to save. See "Public holidays".

### While the month is still running

For a month that has not ended, all four figures cover only the days counted so far, not the whole month.

- On **My time**, under the figures: **"As things stand on Tue 17 Mar. The month is not over."** and, under **To work**, **"of 161:42 for the whole month"**.
- On **Team time**, under the table: **"Figures are as things stand on Tue 17 Mar; the month is not over."** The **To work** tile at the top shows only the month so far. The whole-month figure is not on the tile at all — it appears only if you rest the mouse on a person's **To work** cell in the table, so do not quote the tile as somebody's monthly target.

## A worked example

**Anna. On site, part time. Her working week is Monday to Friday, 7:42 each day.** That is 462 minutes a day. March has 21 days her working week says she works.

**What the month asks of her — To work.**

    21 days × 7:42  =  161:42

One of those 21 days is a public holiday, and two of them she took as leave. **To work stays 161:42.** Neither of those reduces it.

**What she actually put in — Worked.**

| Where it came from                        | Hours      |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Timers on work items                      | 127:30     |
| Hours with no work item (meetings, admin) | 8:00       |
| Two days of leave, at 7:42 each           | 15:24      |
| One public holiday, on a day she works    | 7:42       |
| **Worked**                                | **158:36** |

    127:30 + 8:00 + 15:24 + 7:42  =  158:36

**Her balance.**

    158:36 − 161:42  =  −3:06

The **Balance** reads **-3:06**, in red. She was at a desk on 18 of the 21 days and came out three hours and six minutes short.

**Her running balance.** February closed at **+1:30**. She took no hours off in lieu in March. So once March is closed:

    +1:30 + (−3:06) − 0:00  =  −1:36

Her row on **Team time**, on the day the month is agreed, reads:

    Anna · Handed in · 161:42 · 158:36 · -3:06 · — · [Agree]

The **Running balance** column is a dash because the month is not closed yet. After it is closed, that dash becomes **-1:36**, and April will open on that figure.

The manager presses **Agree**, then **Close**, then downloads the file for the payroll accountant.

## The file for the payroll accountant

On **Team time**, the "…" button at the top right offers **Download as CSV** and **Download as Excel**. The file holds one line per person for the month on screen, and is named after the month, for example `hr-2026-03.xlsx`.

Two things to know about it.

**The State column uses different words from the screen.** The file was written to be read by other programs, so it uses fixed English words that do not change with the language of the screen:

| In the file | On the screen     | Are the figures final? |
| ----------- | ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| `Open`      | **Not handed in** | No                     |
| `Submitted` | **Handed in**     | No                     |
| `Approved`  | **Agreed**        | No                     |
| `Locked`    | **Closed**        | **Yes**                |
| `Reopened`  | **Reopened**      | No                     |

Only `Locked` means the figures are final. Say so when you send the file.

**The download replaces the page you were on.** If it works you will not notice. If it fails — an expired session, a server problem — the application is replaced by a page of raw text in the same tab. Press the browser's back button to get the screen back.

The hours columns in the file are decimal hours to two places: **161:42** is written as `161.7`, and **-3:06** as `-3.1`. The minutes columns beside them are exact. Use the minutes columns for anything you add up.

## The whole cycle on one page

| State             | Who acts next                                                    | Where they act | What they press                                                         | What is refused meanwhile                                          |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Not handed in** | The person themselves                                            | **My time**    | **Hand in** — only after the month has ended and every timer is stopped | Nothing. The month can still be changed.                           |
| **Handed in**     | Somebody who looks after the team, and not the person themselves | **Team time**  | **Agree** — no confirmation, and it cannot be undone                    | Hours, absences and attendance in that month                       |
| **Agreed**        | Somebody who looks after the team, their own month included      | **Team time**  | **Close** — a box asks first                                            | Hours, absences and attendance in that month                       |
| **Closed**        | Nobody, unless something is wrong                                | **Team time**  | **Reopen** — a written reason is required                               | Everything. The figures are frozen and are never worked out again. |
| **Reopened**      | The person themselves, again                                     | **My time**    | **Hand in**, and then the whole cycle again                             | Nothing. The month can be changed again.                           |

The **Still to close** tile at the top of **Team time** counts everybody who is not yet at **Closed** — for example **"3 of 7 people"**. **Not handed in**, **Handed in**, **Agreed** and **Reopened** all count as still to close.

## If you get stuck

- **"What am I meant to press on my own hours?"** — "My time — your own month".
- **"How do I put a meeting in?"** — "Recording hours".
- **"What are the start and end times for?"** — "When you were at work".
- **"Where did this figure come from?"** — "Hours in detail".
- **"How do I read everybody's month?"** — "Team time — everybody's month".
- **"Who is allowed to do what, and how do I add a manager?"** — "People and their terms".
- **"How do absences and requests work?"** — "Time away from work" and "Leave".
- **"Why did a holiday change nothing?"** — "Public holidays".
- **"How do I get old months in?"** — "Bringing hours in from somewhere else".
- **"It gave me a red message I do not understand."** — "When something goes wrong".

If none of those answers it, ask whoever looks after the team. They can reopen a month, settle a flagged day and download the file, and none of those is something you can do wrong in a way that cannot be corrected — every one of them is recorded.
