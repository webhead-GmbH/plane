# Working time — the handbook

Everything about recording working time here, written for people who have never
been shown how. Nothing on these pages assumes you know anything about the
system, and nothing assumes you are comfortable with computers.

You do not have to read it all. Find the page that matches your question, read
that, and stop.

The same handbook is written in [Deutsch](../de/README.md) and
[فارسی](../fa/README.md).

## If you are new here

Read **[Start here](01-start-here.md)** first. It is the only page that assumes
nothing at all: what the system is for, who can see what, what the words on the
screens mean, how a length of time is written down, and what happens to a month
from the day it opens to the day it is finished.

Then read **[My time — your own month](02-my-time.md)**, which is the one screen
you will use every week, and **[Recording hours](03-recording-hours.md)**, which
is how the hours get in there.

That is enough to do your job. The rest is here for when you need it.

## Three things worth knowing before anything else

**A length of time is written with a colon.** `7:42` is seven hours and
forty-two minutes. It is not seven point four two. Where a screen will also
accept `7.7` or `90m`, that page says so.

**A holiday or a day of leave counts as hours worked.** It is added to
**Worked**. It does not lower **To work**, which stays at the hours your working
week says you owe. So a month with a public holiday in it shows the same **To
work** as a month without one, and the holiday shows up on the other side
instead. Your **Balance** comes out the same either way. This is the single
thing most often misread, and [Start here](01-start-here.md) explains it
properly.

**Some things cannot be undone.** Handing a month in, closing a month, removing
a record. Every page says so before it tells you how, and says who can put it
back if anybody can.

## If you get stuck

Read [When something goes wrong](13-when-something-goes-wrong.md) — it lists
every message the screens can put in front of you.

If that does not cover it, ask whoever looks after the team. Nothing here is
urgent enough to guess at: a wrong figure in a month that has been closed is far
more work to undo than a question asked before it was closed.
